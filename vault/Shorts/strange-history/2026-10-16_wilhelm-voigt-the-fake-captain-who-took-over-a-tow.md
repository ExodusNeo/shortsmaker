- Topic: Wilhelm Voigt, the Captain of Köpenick: on 16 October 1906 a jobless ex-convict shoemaker in a second-hand captain's uniform commandeered about ten real soldiers, took a train to Köpenick, occupied the town hall, had the mayor taken away under guard and seized 4,002 marks (writing a receipt). He was caught ten days later, became a press sensation and was pardoned early by Kaiser Wilhelm II in 1908. His likely goal was a passport, but Köpenick town hall had no passport office | Story shape: one person against the odds
