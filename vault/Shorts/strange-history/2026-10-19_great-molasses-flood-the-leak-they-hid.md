- Topic: On 15 January 1919 a giant tank of molasses burst in Boston's North End, sending a sticky wave about 25 feet high through the streets at roughly 35 mph. It flattened buildings and bent elevated railway girders, and rescuers spent days wading through syrup. The twist is that the company blamed anarchist bombers, but a years-long court case found the tank had been badly built and never properly tested; it leaked so much that it was painted brown to hide the leaks.
